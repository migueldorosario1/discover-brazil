The 100 Best Bars in Brazil ranking, produced by Casual Exame and published on August 28, 2025, placed Tan Tan, from São Paulo, in first place for the third consecutive time. The list was formed from votes by 50 critics and influencers, who chose five bars from their region and five from other parts of the country. In total, establishments from 17 cities in 12 states and the Federal District are represented.

Commanded by Thiago Bañares, Tan Tan celebrated ten years with the "Pour-Hibition" menu, which explores three historical moments in the relationship with alcohol. Among the drinks, the Elegance (R$ 70) features shissô, white cachaça, and red vermouth, while the Sinner (R$ 45) is a non-alcoholic option made with corn and house-produced zero-proof rum. Alcohol-free recipes occupy one-third of the menu.

Second place, with 13 votes, went to The Liquor Store, also by Bañares and head bartender Caio Carvalhaes. The bar, with only 25 seats, operates above the Goya Zushi restaurant in the Jardins neighborhood and focuses exclusively on cocktails. The menu brings together 12 signature creations, such as the Flower Power (R$ 62), a clarified hibiscus drink, and the Maria Bonita (R$ 65), served at -14 °C.

In third place, with 12 votes, is Exímia, opened just over a year ago in Itaim Bibi, São Paulo. Bartender Márcio Silva based the menu on anthropology and history research, with drinks like Ameríndio (R$ 49), combining rum, masala, and amburana. Chef Manu Buffara, elected best in Latin America by 50 Best in 2022, signs the dishes, including the grilled bone marrow (R$ 72).

The highest-ranked bar in Rio de Janeiro is Nosso (7th place, 9 votes), in Ipanema, with cocktails such as Espresso da Mata (R$ 44) and Sururu Milk Punch (R$ 42). In Salvador, the couple Fabrício Lemos and Lisiane Arouca placed two addresses: Megiro (6th, 10 votes), which reinvents classics like octopus sarapatel (R$ 92), and MiniBar Gem (7th, 9 votes), with snacks like the vatapá dream (R$ 48). The complete list of the 100 bars is available on the exame.com website.
