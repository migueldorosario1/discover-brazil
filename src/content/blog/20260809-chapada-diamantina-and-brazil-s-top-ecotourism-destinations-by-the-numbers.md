In May 2025, the blog MaladeAventuras published a list of 18 ecotourism destinations in Brazil, drawing on data about protected areas and tourist infrastructure. Chapada Diamantina National Park, in the state of Bahia, covers 70,000 hectares of caatinga vegetation and is home to Cachoeira da Fumaça, a 400-metre waterfall. The town of Lençóis, the main base for visiting the park, sits roughly six hours by car from Salvador.

## Other destinations with notable figures

- **PETAR (SP):** More than 350 catalogued caves in the municipality of Iporanga, according to the state park.
- **Ilhabela (SP):** 360 waterfalls mapped by the local government, alongside beaches such as Bonete and Castelhanos.
- **Nobres (MT):** Located 150 kilometres from Cuiabá, known for snorkelling and floating in crystal-clear rivers.

The blog recommends using shared transport and accredited tour agencies to reduce CO₂ emissions, a trend that gained momentum after the pandemic. Areas such as Fernando de Noronha and Lençóis Maranhenses enforce daily visitor limits and require authorised guides, according to the platform's reports.
