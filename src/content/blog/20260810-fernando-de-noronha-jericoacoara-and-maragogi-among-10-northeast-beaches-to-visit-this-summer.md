Travel search platform data indicates that Brazil's Northeast concentrated the largest share of beach destination searches for the 2025 summer season. A list of 10 beaches in the region, published by tourism portals, brings together destinations such as Fernando de Noronha, Jericoacoara and Maragogi.

The destinations span the states of Pernambuco, Ceará, Rio Grande do Norte, Alagoas and Bahia. Notable features include sandstone cliffs, natural tidal pools and dunes.

## Beaches on the list

- **Praia dos Carneiros (Tamandaré, Pernambuco):** A beachside chapel and coconut groves set the scene, with boat trips available along the coast.

- **Baía do Sancho (Fernando de Noronha, Pernambuco):** Reached by descending a stairway carved between rocks, this beach has been ranked the best in the world in previous international awards.

- **Jericoacoara (Jijoca de Jericoacoara, Ceará):** A landscape of dunes and lagoons, with visits to the Pedra Furada rock formation and sunsets watched from the dunes.

- **Praia de Pipa (Tibau do Sul, Rio Grande do Norte):** Clifftop scenery and resident dolphins are the draw here; Praia do Amor takes on a heart shape when seen from above.

- **Praia do Gunga (Roteiro, Alagoas):** Where the sea meets Lagoa do Roteiro, flanked by cliffs and accessible by buggy tours.

- **Praia do Espelho (Porto Seguro, Bahia):** Reached via an unpaved road, this beach is known for its natural tidal pools used for snorkeling.

- **Maragogi (Alagoas):** The natural pools known as the Galés are sought out for marine life observation, including colorful reef fish.

- **Canoa Quebrada (Aracati, Ceará):** Reddish sandstone cliffs frame the beach, while the village's Rua Broadway offers a lively nightlife scene.

- **Barra de São Miguel (Alagoas):** Reefs create calm natural pools that are popular with families.

- **Praia de Genipabu (Natal, Rio Grande do Norte):** Shifting dunes, buggy rides and dromedary tours make this one of the region's most distinctive beach experiences.

Accommodation across these destinations ranges from guesthouses and boutique pousadas to full-service resorts, with operators offering both nautical and land-based excursions. Local tourism associations report that occupancy rises significantly during the peak summer season, which runs from December through March.
