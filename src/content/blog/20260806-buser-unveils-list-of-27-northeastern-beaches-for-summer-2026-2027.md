The travel platform Buser has put together a guide to 27 Northeastern beaches for the 2026/2027 summer season. The list covers all nine states in the region and selects three beaches per coastline, taking into account profiles such as families, surfers, and travelers seeking peace and quiet. The company recommends checking additional information from official sources such as Visit Brasil.

Destinations with natural pools stand out, such as Porto de Galinhas in Pernambuco and São Miguel dos Milagres in Alagoas. For villages, Canoa Quebrada, 160 km from Fortaleza, and Barra Grande in Piauí are included. The guide also highlights well-structured urban beaches such as Ponta Negra in Natal and Atalaia in Aracaju.

In Maranhão, Praia do Amor in Tutóia is mentioned as a gateway to the Pequenos Lençóis Maranhenses. Praia do Futuro in Fortaleza offers a vast network of beach huts with pools, while Morro Branco in Ceará stands out for its cliffs and natural labyrinth. Buser recommends bus travel as an option for getting around the Northeastern coast.
