The municipality of Bonito, in Mato Grosso do Sul, was the top vote-getter in the ecotourism category of the Best Destinations Award 2024/2025. The survey, conducted among more than 28,000 readers of the Best Destinations website, awarded the city 15.34% of the votes. The destination is known for tourism management in areas of crystalline rivers, caves and waterfalls, and holds the Carbon Neutral seal.

Second place went to Foz do Iguaçu (PR), with 10.74% of the votes. Fernando de Noronha (PE) came in third with 7.17%, followed by Lençóis Maranhenses (MA) with 6.57%. Chapada Diamantina (BA) rounded out the top 5 with 4.41%.

## The 10 most voted destinations

- Bonito (MS) – 15.34%
- Foz do Iguaçu (PR) – 10.74%
- Fernando de Noronha (PE) – 7.17%
- Lençóis Maranhenses (MA) – 6.57%
- Chapada Diamantina (BA) – 4.41%
- Cambará do Sul (RS) – 4.25%
- Brotas (SP) – 4.10%
- Caldas Novas (GO) – 4.05%
- Capitólio (MG) – 3.99%
- Jalapão (TO) – 3.95%

The release of the ranking comes amid an expansion of nature travel offerings in Brazil. The PlanetaEXO platform listed 15 ecotourism destinations for 2026, including some of the places highlighted in the award, such as Lençóis Maranhenses, Chapada Diamantina and Jalapão. The platform's selection also covers regions such as the Amazon, Pantanal and Serra da Capivara.
