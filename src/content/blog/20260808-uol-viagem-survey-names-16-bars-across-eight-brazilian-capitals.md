UOL Viagem published a list of 16 bars and restaurants in eight Brazilian capitals in early January. The selection brings together venues that combine cocktails, food, and music, with settings ranging from themed pubs to historic bistros.

The addresses were chosen for offerings that include signature drinks and craft beers, as well as bar snacks priced from R$ 9.90 to R$ 69. Highlights include venues in Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Salvador, Recife, and other cities.

## Highlights from the list

- **Bar Sobe (Rio de Janeiro):** On the terrace of a mansion in Jardim Botânico, this bar serves drinks such as the Biriba (R$ 26.90), created by bartender William Barão, and queijo coalho sticks (R$ 24.90) with a view of Corcovado.

- **BTNK (São Paulo):** Located in a former train station in Mooca, it has a restaurant car and a drink list created by Fernando Lisboa, including the Howl (R$ 35) with bourbon and Aperol.

- **Haus Lajetop & Beergarden (Recife):** With three spaces and a soundtrack chosen by customers, it offers the Neon Grün (R$ 18), a drink with vodka and green fruits, and bratwurst sausage (R$ 35).

- **Groove Bar (Salvador):** Live rock cover performances and boteco-style snacks, such as a portion of filet mignon with fries (R$ 34.99).

- **Stadt Jever (Belo Horizonte):** A German-style house since 1983, with Wäls Cristal draft beer at R$ 7.40 (300 ml) and pork knuckle with sauerkraut (R$ 59.90).

The full list also includes Biermarkt Vom Fass in Porto Alegre, with 24 types of draft beer and rock classics, and Chá Comigo in Belo Horizonte, which operates without waiters and serves Rooibos Original tea (R$ 9.90) in a laid-back setting.
