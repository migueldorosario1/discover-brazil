Tour operator Acqua Viagens, based in Bonito (Mato Grosso do Sul), reported a 25% increase in bookings for family ecotourism packages to waterfall parks in the first half of 2025. The comparison with the same period in 2024 points to growing demand for destinations that combine easy trails and support infrastructure.

Among the most sought-after destinations are Iguaçu National Park in Foz do Iguaçu (Paraná), Estância Mimosa in Serra da Bodoquena (Mato Grosso do Sul), and Lençóis Maranhenses (Maranhão). In Serra da Bodoquena, the 2.5 km loop trail offers swimming in crystal-clear waters and the chance to spot fish such as lambari and piraputanga, always with a local guide.

Acqua Viagens attributes the growth to the adaptation of attractions for families with children and older adults: paved walkways, panoramic elevators at Garganta do Diabo, and restaurants with high chairs. Chapada Diamantina (Bahia) and Chapada das Mesas (Maranhão) also rank among the ten most requested destinations, home to some of the country's largest waterfalls.

- Iguaçu National Park: access via walkways and elevators, infrastructure with the Porto Canoas restaurant.
- Serra da Bodoquena (Bonito): 2.5 km loop trail at Estância Mimosa, zip line, and complete bathing complex.
- Chapada Diamantina: Cachoeira da Fumaça (380 m) and Gruta da Torrinha among family itineraries.
- Lençóis Maranhenses: circuits in 4x4 vehicles and seasonal lagoons with guide support.

According to the Bonito Tourism Board, the municipality received 310,000 visitors in 2024, around 40% of whom chose itineraries that include waterfalls and river floating. The projection for 2025 indicates that this volume will be surpassed, with advance reservations already 30% above the previous year's figures.
