As the summer high season arrives, tour operator TZ Viagens has published a list of five beaches in the Northeast that are in greatest demand during the holidays. The destinations range from archipelagos with more than 20 islands to villages with natural pools at low tide.

## Destinations selected by the operator

- **Fernando de Noronha (PE):** an archipelago of 21 islands, with beaches such as Baía do Sancho, Baía dos Porcos, Praia do Leão, and Praia do Cachorro. It is possible to watch sea turtle nesting and dive among schools of fish.
- **Jericoacoara (CE):** a village with calm-water beaches and boat tours. Hammocks set in the water are a popular spot. The agency warns that there are no banks in the area and vendors usually only accept cash.
- **Porto de Galinhas (PE):** located in Ipojuca, the beach has warm natural pools and a well-established tourism infrastructure, attracting large numbers of visitors in summer.
- **Arraial d'Ajuda (BA):** a district of Porto Seguro with multicultural shops and a varied restaurant scene. Between July and November, humpback whales can be spotted off the coast.
- **Maragogi (AL):** the reefs form natural pools that are accessible at low tide. Schools of fish approach bathers; local guidance is to check the tide tables.

TZ Viagens says it offers customized itineraries for these five destinations, as announced on its customer service channels.
