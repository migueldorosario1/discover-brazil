Housing platform QuintoAndar published a roundup of bars, restaurants, and live music venues across eight Brazilian state capitals, identifying neighborhoods with the highest concentration of nightlife activity. The selection ranges from long-established institutions to contemporary live music spaces.

## Rio de Janeiro

Lapa leads the Rio listings with venues clustered around the city's iconic arches, including Circo Voador, Fundição Progresso, and Rio Scenarium. The hillside neighborhood of Santa Teresa is represented by Bar do Mineiro and Armazém São Thiago, while Copacabana and Ipanema contribute traditional neighborhood bars such as Pavão Azul and Shenanigans Pub. In Barra da Tijuca, Pineapple Drinkeria and Buteco Seu Rufino appear on Avenidas Olegário Maciel and Armando Lombardi.

## São Paulo

Vila Madalena is the focal point for São Paulo's after-work scene, with Bar Astor, O Pasquim, and Quintal do Espeto among the highlighted happy-hour destinations. The upscale districts of Itaim Bibi and Vila Olímpia are represented by rooftop bars including Baleia Rooftop and Eu Tu Eles Bar, while Pinheiros features Gaarden Bar and Tetto Rooftop Lounge.

## Salvador

Rio Vermelho stands out as Salvador's primary nightlife district, with Blue Praia Bar, Boteco do Paulista, and Parador Z1 Bar among the featured addresses.

## Belo Horizonte

The Savassi neighborhood is described as the city's main hub for bars and pubs, with Laicos Bar, Dalva Botequim Musical, and Redentor Bar among the listings. The traditional neighborhood of Santa Tereza is also noted, represented by Bar do Bolão.

## Porto Alegre and Curitiba

Porto Alegre's Cidade Baixa district features Boteco Pedrini and Divina Comédia, while Curitiba's contribution centers on the Batel neighborhood, with Sheridan's Irish Pub as a key reference.

## Brasília

The capital closes the survey with Asa Sul hosting bars such as Calaf and O'Rilley, and Asa Norte offering options including Carcassonne Pub.

QuintoAndar's mapping does not include visitor flow data or revenue figures for any of the establishments listed.
