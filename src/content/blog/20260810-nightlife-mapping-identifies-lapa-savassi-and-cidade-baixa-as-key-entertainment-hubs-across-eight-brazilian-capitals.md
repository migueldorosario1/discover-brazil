A new mapping of nightlife across eight Brazilian capital cities has organized the main clusters of bars, restaurants and entertainment venues. Released this week, the survey breaks each city down by neighborhood to help visitors navigate their options.

## Rio de Janeiro

In Rio de Janeiro, nighttime activity is concentrated in Lapa, Santa Teresa, Copacabana, Leblon, Barra da Tijuca, Centro and Gávea. Circo Voador and Clube dos Democráticos are among the venues listed for live music programming.

## São Paulo

In São Paulo, the nightlife scene is spread across Vila Madalena, Itaim Bibi, Pinheiros, Jardins, Avenida Paulista, Consolação and Vila Nova Conceição. Bar Astor, Lions Night Club and Kia Ora Pub are among the options featured.

## Salvador

In Salvador, the nightlife circuit covers the neighborhoods of Rio Vermelho, Barra, Ondina and Itapuã. Blue Praia Bar is one of the addresses operating in the area.

## Florianópolis

Florianópolis channels its nighttime crowd toward Lagoa da Conceição, Jurerê and Avenida Beira-Mar Norte. The Black Swan Pub and the samba bar De Raiz are among the listed options.

## Belo Horizonte

In Belo Horizonte, the Santa Tereza, Savassi and Vila da Serra circuit concentrates bars and nightclubs. Venues such as Bar do Bolão, Laicos Bar, Bwana Pub and the club Josefine appear in the survey.

## Porto Alegre

Porto Alegre sustains its nightlife in Cidade Baixa and the Moinhos de Vento neighborhood. Boteco Pedrini, the Divina Comédia space, Feelings Club Bar and Bodega Bar e Danceteria are among the venues listed.

## Curitiba

Curitiba organizes its nighttime commerce around Batel, Largo da Ordem and Rebouças. Sheridan's Irish Pub and the electronic music clubs Vibe, Eon and Pulse are part of the circuit.

## Natal

In Natal, nightlife options are found in Ponta Negra and near Parque das Dunas. The forró venue Arrasta-Pé and Poty Bar are among the survey's recommendations.
