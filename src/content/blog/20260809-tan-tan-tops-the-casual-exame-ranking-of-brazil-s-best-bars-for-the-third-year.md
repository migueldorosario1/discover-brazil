The Casual Exame's '100 Best Bars in Brazil' ranking for 2025 was announced on December 30th. The Tan Tan bar in São Paulo maintained its leadership for the third consecutive year, with 21 votes from a jury of 50 critics and influencers.

The list compiles establishments from 17 cities, 12 states, and the Federal District. Voting considered five regional nominations and five from other regions by each juror, without order of importance.

## The Top Five of 2025
- **1st - Tan Tan (São Paulo)**: A bar managed by Thiago Bañares, featuring a cocktail menu that includes non-alcoholic drinks and a menu of snacks and dishes such as the katsu sando sandwich.
- **2nd - The Liquor Store (São Paulo)**: A 25-seat venue in Jardins, focused on original cocktails and a selection of appetizers.
- **3rd - Exímia (São Paulo)**: A bar in Itaim Bibi with creations by Márcio Silva based on Brazilian ingredients and dishes by chef Manu Buffara, such as grilled bluefin tuna.
- **4th - Santana Bar (São Paulo)**: A cocktail menu developed with the help of artificial intelligence and inspired by fashion, paired with snacks like a shrimp club sandwich.
- **5th - Caledonia Whisky & Co. (São Paulo)**: Specializing in whisky, offering a menu with 16 harmonization experiences between original cocktails and dishes such as rye tartare.

Outside the top 5, the ranking highlighted newcomers like Megiro in Salvador, a casual bar with reinterpretations of typical Bahian dishes, opened a year ago by the partners of MiniBar Gem. In previous editions, venues such as Bar da Dona Onça in Copan and SubAstor in São Paulo, as well as Rio de Janeiro bars like Nosso and Bar Urca, have also been featured.

The 2025 list reinforces the concentration of São Paulo addresses in the top positions but includes a variety of cocktail bars, botecos, and gastrobars in different regions of the country. The complete ranking is available on the Casual Exame website.
